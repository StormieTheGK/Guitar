🧠 FIRST THINGS FIRST

What is a fretboard?

The fretboard is the long part of your guitar where you put your fingers.

🎸

Think of it like a map!

Every fret can play a different note. That might sound like a lot to learn...

But don't worry! You don't have to remember everything at once.

We will learn some easy patterns that help you find the notes.

🗺️

⭐ STEP 1

Learn your 6 strings!

A guitar has six strings. Each string has a name.

From the thickest string to the thinnest, remember:

E A D G B E
💡 Say it out loud: E - A - D - G - B - E

🎸 Meet the Strings

A 5th string
D 4th string
G 3rd string

⭐ The first and last strings are both E!
